# Feature Specification: AI Assistance And Forecasting

## Feature Overview

Provide global KAM AI, AI Timeline Search, AI Account Briefs, AI Stage Prediction, predictive forecast charts, and AI Task Summary with source citations, confidence, disclaimers, and human-review controls.

## Business Goal

Accelerate insight discovery and account preparation while keeping AI advisory, permission-aware, source-backed, and non-authoritative without explicit human confirmation.

## User Roles

- All Users
- Account Manager / KAM
- KAM Head / VP
- Leadership Viewer / Executive
- Admin
- Platform

## User Stories Covered

- Story 19.1 - Global KAM AI Query Dashboard
- Story 19.2 - AI Timeline Search
- Story 19.3 - AI Account Briefs
- Story 19.4 - AI Stage Prediction and predictive forecast charts
- Story 19.5 - AI Task Summary

## Functional Requirements

- Provide persistent bottom-right KAM AI launcher across primary screens.
- Support authorized account selector, scope toggles, suggested prompts, recent query history, structured answer cards, citations, confidence, and disclaimer.
- Scope toggles: Timeline, Opportunities, Governance, Notes, KYC, and Document Search off by default.
- Configure AI Search business vocabulary, synonyms, searchable fields, default business terms, and object eligibility; default vocabulary includes red account, at risk, strategic account, inactive account, and growth-ready.
- Support semantic search over notes, charters, SOWs, attachments, and uploaded/linked documents, with page/section citations where available and parent permission context.
- Support structured AI Timeline Search intents and semantic fallback ranking.
- Handoff from Timeline Search to KAM AI with query and account context.
- Generate AI Account Briefs: Account Brief, Period Summary, Pre-Meeting Brief, Risk Narrative.
- Cache AI Account Briefs for one hour per account/type and support manual refresh.
- Store thumbs-up/down feedback.
- Allow authorized edit-to-timeline-note flow preserving original AI draft.
- Provide AI Stage Prediction with predicted stage, confidence, basis, contributing factors, citations, gaps, and recommended actions.
- Provide predictive charts for six-month ARR projection, health trend, and modeled pipeline.
- Forecast chart inputs include current ARR, commercial exposure from approved KYC/SOW records, open pipeline value, opportunity stage distribution, health score trajectory, active risks/signals, and timeline events from the last 90 days.
- Provide AI Task Summary card on AM Home with task counts, urgent/due items, high-load accounts, stale indicator, and disclaimer.

## Non-Functional Requirements

- AI/keyword search standard results should return within 5 seconds where backend services are healthy.
- AI retrieval must apply RBAC before context reaches the AI gateway.
- AI failures must not block core workflows.
- Every factual AI claim must cite source records or state data is insufficient.
- Non-dismissible disclaimer appears on all AI output surfaces.

## Permissions & Authorization

- All authenticated users can query AI only over authorized accounts, scopes, records, and fields.
- Admin configures AI vocabulary, synonyms, searchable fields, object eligibility, and reviews feedback/logs.
- KAM/KAM Head/Leadership Viewer generate briefs and forecasts where account access exists.
- KAM Head approves stage change; AI prediction cannot mutate stage.
- Leadership/Admin edit-to-timeline-note where configured.

## Validation Rules

- Query text is required.
- Account context required for account-scoped AI.
- Document Search requires explicit per-session enablement.
- Period Summary requires date range.
- Stage prediction cannot update account stage directly.
- Forecasts must display missing/low-confidence gaps.
- AI Task Summary uses only authenticated user's assigned account data.
- Disclaimer must render before output is considered complete.

## Search Requirements

- Search account selector.
- Search query history.
- AI Timeline Search supports structured intent recognition and semantic search.
- AI answer citations are searchable within generated output where UI supports it.
- Admin can search AI vocabulary terms, synonyms, searchable fields, and AI audit logs.

## Filter Requirements

- KAM AI scope toggles filter retrieval context.
- AI Timeline Search uses timeline filters plus recognized intents.
- Brief history filters: type, date range, generated by, feedback.
- Prediction history filters: account, generated by, date.
- Forecast input can include horizon/date range where supported.
- AI vocabulary filters: active state, object type, field, term category.

## Sort Requirements

- Query history sorted newest first.
- Timeline semantic results ranked by relevance.
- Brief and prediction history sorted newest first.
- AI Task Summary accounts sorted by current task load/urgency.

## Pagination Requirements

- Query history is paginated.
- Timeline AI search results are paginated.
- AI brief history is paginated.
- Prediction history is paginated.
- AI audit logs and vocabulary lists are paginated.

## UI Requirements

- Global launcher and AI panel with account selector, scope toggles, query input, suggested prompts, history, and answer cards.
- Admin AI vocabulary/searchability configuration screen.
- Timeline AI answer cards with detected intent, structured/semantic labels, citations, and KAM AI handoff.
- AI Account Brief card with type selector, date range, cache timestamp, refresh, feedback, edit/save-to-timeline.
- Account stage prediction card with evidence, gaps, confidence, and stage-change workflow entry.
- Forecast chart cards with baseline, projection, trend line, inputs, gaps, and disclaimer.
- AI Task Summary card with counts, links, refresh timestamp, stale indicator, and disclaimer.

## Loading States

- AI answer streaming/loading.
- Query history loading.
- Timeline AI search loading.
- Brief generation/refresh loading.
- Stage prediction and forecast loading.
- AI Task Summary refresh loading.

## Empty States

- No AI query history.
- No authorized accounts.
- No timeline AI results.
- No generated briefs.
- No prediction history.
- No tasks/signals for AI Task Summary.

## Error States

- AI gateway unavailable.
- No authorized retrieval context.
- Insufficient data.
- Citation source restricted or unavailable.
- Forecast input gaps too severe.
- Brief save-to-timeline denied.

## Edge Cases

- Switching account resets active query scope and loads selected account history.
- AI must not reveal hidden records or hidden-record counts.
- Cached brief must show generated timestamp and cache status.
- Edited brief timeline note distinguishes original AI draft and human-edited version.
- Forecast charts do not auto-populate authoritative records.
- AI Task Summary failure does not block AM Home dashboard.

## Missing Requirements

- Exact AI model/provider behavior is not specified.
- Prompt templates and answer schemas are not specified.
- Confidence scoring method is not defined.
- Exact AI vocabulary governance workflow and approval rules are not specified.
- Rate limits, token limits, and cost controls are unspecified.

## Ambiguous Requirements

- "All platform screens" for global launcher needs a definitive screen list.
- Which leadership/admin roles can edit AI briefs is configuration-dependent and unclear.
- Forecast horizon defaults and chart types are not fully defined.

## Conflicting Requirements

- AI can produce forecast charts but must not surface them in executive reports/dashboards/exports without explicit action; UI must ensure forecast outputs remain scoped unless confirmed.

## Unspecified Edge Cases

- Concurrent AI generations for same account/type.
- Deleting AI query history.
- Handling hallucinated citation IDs from AI gateway.
- Failed cache refresh with existing cached brief.
- Stage prediction generated from stale data.

## Audit/Logging Requirements

- Log AI queries, interpreted filters, scopes, accessed records, source context, model/run metadata, confidence, failures, and citations.
- Audit AI brief generation, cache hit/refresh, feedback, edit-to-timeline, and original/edited versions.
- Audit stage prediction generation and any user-initiated stage workflow from prediction.
- Log forecast inputs, gaps, and output visibility.
- Log AI Task Summary refresh and stale state.

## Test Scenarios

- Open KAM AI from multiple screens and preserve current page.
- Query authorized account data and verify citations.
- Attempt query against unauthorized account and verify account not selectable.
- Enable Document Search and verify retrieval only then includes documents.
- Run structured Timeline Search intent and semantic fallback.
- Handoff Timeline Search to KAM AI.
- Generate each AI brief type and verify cache behavior.
- Save edited AI brief as timeline note.
- Generate stage prediction and verify it cannot mutate stage directly.
- Generate forecast chart and verify it does not enter authoritative records.
- Refresh AI Task Summary and verify stale indicator.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AI features are accessible, source-backed, permission-aware, and clearly labeled.
- AI output never becomes authoritative without explicit human workflow.
- Search, brief, forecast, and summary surfaces handle loading, empty, and error states.
- AI audit logs are sufficient for Admin review.

## Added From Technical Logic Document

- All AI outputs must be clearly labeled as AI-assisted and include confidence, limitations, citations, insufficient-data indicators where applicable, and a non-dismissible disclaimer.
- AI retrieval must apply RBAC, account access, field-level security, and sensitivity filtering before any context reaches the AI/LLM Gateway.
- AI must not write official records, approve records, change account stage, create signals, create escalations, execute playbooks, or mutate tasks without an explicit human workflow and authorization.
- AI failures must not block core manual workflows. Each AI surface must provide retry/manual fallback or clear insufficient-data state.
- Global KAM AI panel must include persistent launcher, account context selector, scope toggles for Timeline, Opportunities, Governance, Notes, KYC, and Documents, suggested prompts, per-account query history, and structured answer cards.
- AI answer cards must include detected intent, confidence, disclaimer, citations/source routes, limitation notes, and redaction/insufficient-data messaging when context is missing or restricted.
- Query history must be stored per account/user where enabled and must be deletable or governed by retention policy.
- AI Timeline Search must support structured intent recognition plus semantic/text fallback, result ranking, citations, redaction metadata, and "Try in KAM AI" handoff with query/account context.
- AI Account Briefs must be generated from approved KYC, approved engagement/SOW records, current health, active signals, opportunities, governance context, escalations, recent timeline, and authorized notes/documents.
- Saving an edited AI brief to timeline must preserve original AI draft, human-edited version, editor, timestamp, citations, and disclaimer context.
- Stage Prediction inputs must include KYC confidence/completeness, approved SOW/renewal terms, recent timeline from the last 90 days, pipeline/stage distribution, current health/RAG, funding context, and active risks/signals.
- Stage Prediction output must include predicted stage, confidence, basis, factors/evidence, influences, gaps, and recommended actions.
- Stage Prediction can only initiate a standard human stage-change workflow; it must never directly change the official account stage.
- Forecast charts must be directional and must not auto-populate official records, dashboards, exports, executive reports, or financial reporting unless an authorized user explicitly confirms the output for that destination.
- Forecast chart inputs must include six-month ARR projection inputs, health trend, remaining modeled pipeline, approved commercial/SOW context, opportunity stage distribution, active risk/signals, and recent timeline context.
- Forecast outputs must show baseline, projection, assumptions, missing/low-confidence gaps, cited inputs, and disclaimer.
- AI Task Summary must use only the authenticated user's assigned/authorized account data and must refresh independently from the rest of AM Home.
